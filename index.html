The Framework
What is AntiGravity?
Based on Newton's Law of Gravity applied to human behaviour. Two forces determine how much your habits and patterns control you — psychological mass (your attachments and habits) and distance from triggers (how close you are to what pulls you).

This app maps your gravitational field and generates the opposing force to escape it.
"Neither falling. Nor drifting. The balance is your true self."
Dr. Shadi Obeidat

Field Mode — ORBIT
Capture moments as they happen — any trigger, emotion, or thought that pulls you. The AI reads each moment and tells you:

◈ Which of your 6 operating modes is active (your Mass Layer)
◈ Which gravitational domain is heaviest (Thinking, Emotional, Behavioral, Physical)
◈ A personalised Stop Signal — one question to interrupt the pattern
◈ How this moment connects to your Desired Orbit
💡 Capture at least 3 moments to activate your Mass Layer distribution in the Report.

Screen 2 of 3
Mirror Mode — Verbal
After a significant interaction — a difficult meeting, a tense conversation — speak freely for 60–90 seconds. Don't prepare. The system reads your language patterns, not your facts.

◈ Detects Meta-Model violations in your language
◈ Scores your Gravity (0–100) and Mask Score (authenticity gap)
◈ Your Voice Coach responds in your own register — then leads toward lightness
◈ Generates a Voice Letter written in your own linguistic fingerprint
💡 You can type instead of speaking if microphone access is unavailable.

Daily Practice
The Stop Signal
The most powerful feature. Random alerts fire throughout your day — calibrated to your dominant gravitational domain — to catch you inside the gravitational field at the moment it fires.

This is not a reminder. It is an interruption. Real self-observation is only possible mid-automatism — not after the fact.

25 signals across 5 domains. Never the same twice in sequence.
⚡ Tap the "Stop" button in the top bar anytime to trigger a signal manually.

The Field Report
Your unified intelligence report — synthesising everything ORBIT and Verbal Mirror have detected about your gravitational field.

◈ Field Verdict — your primary gravity profile
◈ Primary Domain — the heaviest mass domain
◈ Mass Layer — dominant operating mode and its cost
◈ Escape Signal — one specific antigravity practice
◈ Orbit Statement — one sentence connecting all findings to your desired orbit
💡 Generate after at least 3 field moments and 1 voice session for a meaningful report.

Your First Session
How to begin
Three steps to your first meaningful reading:

1Go to Field Mode and capture one moment happening right now — any trigger or emotion present in your day.
2Go to Mirror Mode and speak for 60 seconds about a recent difficult interaction. Don't prepare.
3Go to Report and generate your first Field Intelligence Report.
The system gets more accurate over time. Every moment you capture narrows the field map.
