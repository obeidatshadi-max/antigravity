How to Use Verbal Mirror

Verbal Mirror combines real voice acoustics (pitch, pace, hesitation) with verbal pattern analysis to produce a complete psychological portrait — including a Gravity Score, Mask Score™, and a Voice Letter written in your own linguistic fingerprint.

Three Modes
Solo Analysis
Speak or type 3–10 sentences about any situation. The app measures your acoustic profile and returns a full personality portrait: Gravity Score (Thinking / Emotional / Behavioral-Physical), Mask Score™, and a Voice Letter.
Conversation Comparison
Enter samples from two people. See side-by-side orbital maps, who is adapting to whom, power dynamics, and where dissonance hotspots live in the conversation.
Voice Journal
60-second daily voice check-in. Tracks your Gravity Score drift over time, pitch trends, and streak. After 7 entries, generates a Weekly Portrait of how your inner state has been moving.
Key Outputs
Gravity Score — Three domains scored 0–10: Thinking (cognitive mass), Emotional (feeling patterns), Behavioral/Physical (action habits). Based on Shadi Obeidat's Antigravity framework.
Mask Score™ — Measures the gap between what you say and how you acoustically deliver it. Three masks: Confidence, Minimization, Obligation. The higher the score, the more your voice and words diverge.
Voice Letter — A three-movement letter written in your own linguistic fingerprint: Mirror (what the voice heard), Gap (what lives between words and delivery), Invitation (one precise thing to shift).
Tips for Best Results
→ Record voice rather than typing — acoustics are only measured from live recording
→ Speak naturally for 30–90 seconds about a real situation, not a prepared speech
→ Choose a moment that had some emotional weight — bland content produces bland analysis
→ If arriving from ORBIT, your gravity profile is pre-loaded — the analysis will be more precise
